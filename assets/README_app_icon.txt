Place your app icon image here as `app_icon.png` (recommended PNG, square, at least 1024x1024).

Steps:
1. Replace this file by your image named `app_icon.png` at this path: `assets/app_icon.png`.
2. Run:
   flutter pub get
   flutter pub run flutter_launcher_icons:main

This will generate launcher icons for Android and iOS from your image.
